About this game

Help sleepy cats find their perfect nap spot! Seat exactly one cat on every coloured cushion, one in each row and column — and remember, cats never sit next to each other, not even diagonally. Every puzzle has exactly one solution you can reach by pure logic, no guessing needed.

  • Learn in 30 seconds with a friendly interactive tutorial
  • 12 handcrafted levels from 5×5 to 7×7 with fresh twists: toy blockers, timed naps, bonus treats and a one-heart challenge
  • 5 cozy worlds: living room, zen garden, beach café, winter cabin and a spooky Halloween night
  • Boosters: Catnip hint, Peek, Undo and Fish Shield
  • Out of hints? Play Treat Catch to earn free Catnip
  • Juicy animations, purrs and confetti on every move
